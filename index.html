ABOUT MEDAI

A smarter way to understand your health concerns.

MedAI uses artificial intelligence and a structured medical knowledge base to help users make sense of symptoms before seeking professional medical care.

Conversational

Describe your symptoms naturally instead of filling out complicated medical forms.

AI-Assisted

The system analyzes reported symptoms and identifies possible health conditions.

Safety Focused

Potential warning signs are identified so users can be directed toward appropriate care.

WHAT MEDAI CAN DO

From symptoms to useful guidance.

Symptom Analysis

Identify and organize symptoms described by the user.

Follow-up Questions

Ask relevant questions to gather additional information.

Possible Conditions

Present conditions that may be consistent with the reported symptoms.

Safety Alerts

Detect potential warning signs requiring professional attention.

Important Health Disclaimer

MedAI is an educational and informational assistant. It does not provide a medical diagnosis and does not replace a qualified healthcare professional. If you have severe, worsening, or emergency symptoms, seek professional medical care immediately.
